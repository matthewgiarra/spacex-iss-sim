DRAGON DOCKING CHALLENGE

Dock SpaceX's Dragon with the International Space Station as fast as you can, with AI on your side. Every challenge has its own leaderboard, plus an overall board.

LEADERBOARD
BEGINNER

AI CAPCOM

You fly. An AI capsule communicator watches your telemetry and talks you in, and it remembers your previous attempts.

  • Pick a callsign and an AI model to be your CAPCOM, or none to fly solo.
  • Fly with the keyboard or the on-screen buttons. Radio CAPCOM questions any time.
  • Try different models: which one makes you fastest?
Needs: this browser tab. Nothing to install.
FLY WITH AI CAPCOM
INTERMEDIATE

Role Reversal

An AI pilot flies, and you're its CAPCOM. It's not very good on its own. Your coaching is what gets it docked.

  • Watch the pilot fly and radio advice. You get 10 calls per flight, so make them count.
  • Between flights, read the pilot's debrief and write standing orders it follows next time.
  • Rule: closing speed must stay under 0.1 + 0.02 × range m/s.
Needs: this browser tab. Nothing to install.
BE THE CAPCOM
ADVANCED

Black Box Dragon

Your AI agent flies over HTTP, but the thrusters are unlabeled and scrambled. It has to work out what each one does, then dock. The clock runs the whole time.

  • Start a mission here to get a private API URL for your agent.
  • 14 thrusters, T01-T14: different strengths, and some may be dead.
  • Sensors give position and attitude only. Speeds must be worked out.
  • Mission time counts sim seconds across every attempt until you dock.
Needs: an AI coding agent (e.g. Claude Code) and Python or curl on your laptop.
